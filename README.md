## The Story

While looking through your late grandmother's old photo album, you discover that she hid a mysterious treasure somewhere inside her abandoned mansion.

Armed with nothing but an old lantern, you step inside to uncover the truth. But your lantern only lasts **20 seconds**, and when its light fades, the mansion becomes far more dangerous.

Search every room, unlock new areas, collect the scattered map pieces, and keep your lantern alive by finding candles and wax before it's too late.

---

## Features

- A unique 20-second lantern mechanic
- Explore a haunted mansion full of secrets
- Find keys to unlock hidden rooms
- Collect map pieces to reveal the treasure
- Extend your lantern by collecting candles and wax
- Survive the darkness and escape with the treasure
